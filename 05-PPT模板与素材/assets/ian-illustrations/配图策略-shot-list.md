## 图 1：核心定位 - 两层分工协作

**放置位置**：Slide 2「手册定位」右侧或底部空白区

**完整提示词**：

```
Generate one standalone 16:9 horizontal Chinese article illustration.

Visual DNA:
Pure white background. Minimalist black hand-drawn line art. Slightly wobbly pen lines. Lots of empty white space. Sparse red/orange/blue handwritten Chinese annotations. Clean absurd product-sketch feeling. No gradients, no shadows, no paper texture, no complex background, no commercial vector style, no PPT infographic look, no cute mascot poster.

Recurring IP character required:
小黑, a small solid-black absurd creature with white dot eyes, tiny thin legs, blank serious expression, slightly uneven hand-drawn body shape. 小黑 must perform the core conceptual action, not decorate the scene.

Theme:
Huawei providing underlying cockpit capability + Aibolian doing upper-layer scene orchestration - a collaboration not replacement relationship

Structure type:
Concept metaphor

Core idea:
A two-tier structure. Bottom tier represents Huawei basic capability (map, voice, app ecosystem). Top tier represents Aibolian scene orchestration (HMI, agent, compliance). Xiao Hei stands between them connecting both tiers.

Composition:
Simple two-tier shelf drawn in black thin hand-drawn lines. Bottom shelf labeled with basic capability elements. Top shelf with scene elements floating above. Xiao Hei in the middle, one hand reaching down to hold bottom shelf, one hand reaching up to adjust floating elements on top shelf. Orange arrows flow upward. Very sparse, lots of white space.

Suggested elements:
Thin hand-drawn shelves / Xiao Hei in middle / small floating boxes on top / orange upward arrows

Chinese handwritten labels:
底层能力 (in black) / 场景编排 (in black) / 接口范围 (in blue) / 协作不替代 (in red)

Color use:
Black for main line art and Xiao Hei. Orange for main flow arrows. Red only for 协作不替代. Blue only for 接口范围.

Constraints:
One image one core structure. Main subject 40-50% of canvas. At least 40% blank white space. At most 5 short handwritten labels. No title in top-left corner. No structure type name on image. Not a formal diagram or slide.
```
